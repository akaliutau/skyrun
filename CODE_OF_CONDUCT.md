# Code of conduct

Skyrun welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat participants with respect, give constructive feedback, and focus discussion
on the work. Assume good intent while taking concerns about harmful behavior
seriously.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private
information without consent are unacceptable. This applies to issues, pull
requests, reviews, and other project spaces, including conduct while representing
the project elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. They should explain
the reason when appropriate and consider the impact and context of the behavior.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact methods
listed on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/akaliutau). Avoid posting
personal details publicly. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and seek a
fair resolution. Participants who need to challenge a decision can contact the
repository owner with the relevant context.
